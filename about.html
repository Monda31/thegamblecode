About The Gamble Code: method and sources

Updated 2026-11-13

The Gamble Code publishes educational guides about roulette betting plans, risk and bankroll control, in English and Italian.

What we are

A small independent publisher of PDF guides and comic guides on roulette staking plans, sold on Payhip and Etsy. We do not run a betting service, take bets, offer prizes or work with any casino.

What we do not claim

  • No method guarantees winnings. Every standard roulette bet has a negative expected value: -2.70% on a European wheel, -5.26% on an American wheel.
  • A betting plan can cap how much a session costs. It cannot change the average result.

How we calculate

  • Probabilities are exact: pockets covered divided by 37 (European) or 38 (American). Payouts follow the standard table.
  • House edge is 1 - (payout-adjusted probability): 1/37 = 2.70% and 2/38 = 5.26%.
  • Streaks: the probability of n losses in a row on an even-money bet is (19/37)n for a given starting spin. Probabilities of a streak somewhere in 100 spins come from our own simulation of 200,000 runs.
  • Martingale and progression tables are arithmetic (Fibonacci sums, doubling). The Martingale bankroll test simulated 200 European-wheel spins from a 1,000 bankroll and a base bet of 10, 20,000 runs. These simulations are ours and are not a published study.

Where we are cautious

Historical anecdotes such as the 1913 Monte Carlo streak are repeated in many books but we have not checked them against a primary source, and the articles say so by how they are phrased.
