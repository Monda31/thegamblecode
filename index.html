Roulette betting plans, explained without the hype

Fixed rules, a known maximum risk and a stop-loss, written for beginners. No promise of winning, because roulette has a house edge and no plan removes it.

Shop the guidesFree session tracker (PDF)

Honest first. On a European wheel the casino keeps about 2.7% of every euro bet in the long run (5.26% on an American wheel). A betting plan can limit how much you risk per session. It cannot turn a losing game into a winning one.

The four methods

Cycle Strike

A step-by-step plan with fixed rules, a stop-loss, a maximum risk of 66 units per attack and a tally sheet.

Ghost Echo

Built on repeat numbers: a "ghost" is a number that hits twice within 15 spins, followed by a capped attack of at most 12 spins.

Fibonacci Strike

A Fibonacci-style chip progression applied per number, with a hard cap of 99 chips at risk per attack.

Santo Graal

Works on the 36 columns of the table, playing at most 12 numbers per column.
